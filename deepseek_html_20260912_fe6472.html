Command center — how to use it

A one-page map. It's a scaffold for time-blindness and task-initiation, not a project manager. Nothing here is graded, and it syncs across your devices.

The day in five moves

1 Commit 3

Hit Choose focus. Pick up to three from Do now. Empty slots are fine. This is the whole plan.

›
2 Start one

Press ▶ on a focus card. A 25-minute block runs. Keep browsing — the timer keeps its own time.

›
3 When you stick

Hit I'm stuck or Body double 5 min. The coach narrows to one 60-second action.

›
4 Drift?

Tap I drifted. It shows what you were doing. No lecture, no streak lost, no penalty.

›
5 Close the day

Hit Enough for today. The page dims. Tomorrow starts clean — nothing carries a penalty.

The whole loop: three tasks, one 25-minute block, close the day. Everything else in the tool is optional on top of that.

Things it does differently

⏱️ Timers that tell the truth

Every countdown is anchored to a real wall-clock end time. Switch tabs, close the laptop — the timer is still right when you come back.

↩ Drift is expected

There's a whole button for it. Returning is treated as normal. No streak to break, no chain to lose, no daily reset that erases yesterday.

🛑 Stopping is a supported action

"Enough for today" is a first-class button, not the absence of activity. The day closes with a note, and reopens in one tap if you change your mind.

💡 Every routine has a "why?"

Tap it on any care card. It shows the actual reason, and what happens if you skip. Built for a brain that needs the logic before it moves.

📊 Retrospective, not a report card

History reads your completion data and extrapolates what consistency actually bought you — calories avoided, minutes of sleep gained, hours of attention rebuilt.

☁ One dashboard, three devices

Syncs through a private gist you own. Edit on laptop, open on iPad, refresh on phone — same state, last-write-wins.

Sync — what it does and doesn't

How to turn it on

  1. Click ☁ Sync top-right
  2. Paste your GitHub token (starts with ghp_)
  3. Paste the gist ID — the hex string at the end of the gist URL
  4. Tick Enable sync
  5. Click Test — should say "Connected"
  6. Click Save

Button states

☁ Sync (plain) — off. Nothing is saving to cloud.
↻ Syncing — pushing right now. Momentary.
☁ Synced (green) — this device and the gist match.
⚠ Sync error (red) — click it. Usually token or gist ID. Rarely, GitHub being slow.
One honest limit: the Pixel Watch can't run this. Nothing designed for a browser will. If you want your Top 3 on your wrist, the realistic path is to mirror the dashboard into Google Tasks or Todoist, then have the watch show those. That's a separate project.

When it goes sideways

Can't start

Tap Make it smaller. It gives you the first 60 seconds of a specific task, not a pep talk.

Overwhelmed

Tap Slow breaths. 4-6-8 protocol, six rounds. Stop any time.

Task won't move

Open it (double-click in Matrix) → Upgrade to project → Break into steps. Eight small actions beat one big one.

It's bigger than tasks

Type what's real into the coach. If it's crisis language, it stops coaching and shows real numbers. That's not a feature — it's the floor.

One rule: the tool works when you open it, and doesn't when you don't. That's not a moral failure — it's the actual constraint. If three days go by without opening it, open it today. Nothing is lost, nothing is behind.
